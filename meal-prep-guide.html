🍗Garlic Soy Marinade
For: Garlic Soy Chicken Rice Bowls

Whisk together, toss with 225g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cover and refrigerate. Allow at least 30 minutes. The longer it marinates, the deeper the flavour.

🧊 Raw marinating chicken keeps up to 2 days in the fridge. Cooking Sunday night

🌶️Chili Sesame Sauce
For: Garlic Soy Chicken Rice Bowls

Whisk until combined.

🧊 Keeps ~1 week in a sealed jar in the fridge

🍅Tuscan Spice Mix
For: Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Combine in a small jar. On Tuesday, add it to the pan with the sun-dried tomatoes and ½ tbsp tomato paste.

🧊 Dry mix, keeps for weeks in an airtight container

🍗 Garlic Soy Chicken Rice Bowls: 2 serves, Sunday＋

Prep Ahead (Sunday afternoon)

  • Minced garlic, grated ginger, chopped spring onions, washed coriander and cucumber (see Vegetable Prep above)
  • Garlic soy marinade on the chicken, plus the chili sesame sauce (see Sauce & Marinade Prep above)

Leave Until Cooking Day

  • Cook the jasmine rice fresh (~⅓ cup uncooked gives 1 cup cooked)
  • Grill or pan-fry the chicken over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes per side, until 75°C inside. Rest it, then slice into strips
  • Mix the sesame rice: 1 cup cooked rice, ½ tbsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp spring onions and 2 tbsp freshly chopped coriander
  • Slice the cucumber just before serving so it stays crisp
  • Assemble: rice, chicken and cucumber, drizzled with chili sesame sauce and topped with the remaining spring onions and chilli flakes (optional)
🍗 Creamy Tuscan Chicken: 2 serves, Tuesday＋

Prep Ahead (Sunday)

  • Chopped shallot, crushed garlic, chopped sun-dried tomatoes and washed parsley (see Vegetable Prep above)
  • Tuscan spice mix (see Sauce & Marinade Prep above)

Leave Until Cooking Day

  • Season the 2 chicken thighs and dust them in 1 tbsp cornflour just before cooking. Done early, the coating goes gluey and won't crisp
  • Fry the chicken in 1 tbsp sun-dried tomato oil and ½ tsp butter for 5–6 minutes skin-side down, then 4–5 minutes on the other side
  • Make the sauce: shallot, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, spice mix, ½ tbsp tomato paste, 75ml chicken stock and 50ml Elmlea single light cream. Simmer the chicken in it for 8–10 minutes
  • Chop the parsley and stir it through at the end so it stays bright
  • Cook 250g basmati rice fresh (~85g uncooked), then serve with grated Parmesan and extra parsley
