🐟 Bang Bang Shrimp — 2 serves＋

Prep Ahead (Sunday)

  • Bang bang sauce and dry batter — see Sauce & Marinade Prep above

Leave Until Cooking Day

  • Buttermilk soak — ⅔ cup buttermilk + ⅓ tbsp sriracha with 265g prawns, only needs 20 minutes, do it fresh
  • Battering and frying the prawns — must be done just before eating so they stay crispy
  • Tossing prawns in bang bang sauce and garnishing with chives/spring onion and chilli oil — assemble right before serving
🥩 Cheat's Beef Pho — 2 serves＋

Prep Ahead (Sunday)

  • 2 garlic cloves for the broth — see Vegetable Prep above
  • Sliced red onion, coriander, and lime wedges — see Vegetable Prep above

Leave Until Cooking Day (broth, noodles, and beef not prepped ahead this week, by request)

  • Cook and rinse 100g flat thin rice noodles — cook per packet instructions minus 2 minutes, rinse in cold water, drain
  • Make the broth: 1 litre low-sodium beef stock, 2 garlic cloves (from veg prep), 15g ginger, 1 star anise, 2 whole cloves, ½ tbsp fish sauce, ½ tbsp brown sugar — simmer 18 minutes, strain out the aromatics
  • Par-freeze 150g beef eye fillet ~30 minutes, then slice as thinly as possible (1–2mm)
  • Dropping the sliced beef into hot broth for 30 seconds — do this fresh so it finishes cooking properly when served
  • 1 handful bean sprouts — very perishable, buy/wash close to cooking day rather than prepping Sunday
  • Final assembly and garnishing (bean sprouts, coriander, lime, red onion, 30ml hoisin, sriracha)
🍗 Chipotle Honey Chicken Bowl — 2 serves＋

Prep Ahead (Sunday)

  • Chicken marinade and green sauce — see Sauce & Marinade Prep above

Leave Until Cooking Day

  • Cooking the chicken — best done fresh for proper caramelisation and texture
  • Cooking the ½ cup jasmine rice
  • Avocado/guacamole (1 tbsp per bowl) — prep just before serving so it doesn't brown
  • Pico de gallo (1 tbsp per bowl) — best made fresh so it doesn't turn watery
