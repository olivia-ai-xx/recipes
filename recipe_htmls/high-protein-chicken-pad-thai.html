High Protein Chicken Pad Thai

↗ View original recipe
Servings
2
Time
~35 min
Main Ingredients
Chicken, rice noodles, tamarind sauce
Calories
~560 kcal / serve
🥬 Ingredients
Chicken Marinade
  • 400g chicken breast, cubed
  • 10g soy sauce (~½ tbsp)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp olive oil, for cooking
Pad Thai Sauce
  • 15g soy sauce (~¾ tbsp)
  • 20g tamarind paste
  • 15g honey
  • 2.5g fish sauce (~½ tsp)
  • 8–10g sriracha (~1½–2 tsp)
  • ½–1 lime, juiced
To Cook
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • ½ medium onion, chopped
  • 1 spring onion, chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 200g cooked rice noodles
To Serve
  • Extra spring onion, sliced
  • Bean sprouts, a small handful
  • 15g crushed peanuts
  • Pinch of chilli flakes
  • Lime wedge
🍳 Recipe
  1. Combine the chicken with the soy sauce, black pepper, garlic powder and chilli flakes. Toss to coat and set aside to marinate while you prep everything else.
  2. Whisk together the pad thai sauce ingredients (soy sauce, tamarind paste, honey, fish sauce, sriracha, lime juice) in a small bowl. It should be a little thick — add a touch more tamarind paste if it looks too watery.
  3. Cook the rice noodles according to packet instructions, then drain and set aside.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat. Cook the marinated chicken for 5–6 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  5. In the same pan, sauté the garlic and onion for 2–3 minutes until softened and fragrant.
  6. Push everything to one side of the pan, crack in the eggs and scramble until just set, then mix through.
  7. Add the cooked noodles, chicken and spring onion back into the pan. Pour over the pad thai sauce and toss well to coat everything evenly.
  8. Divide between bowls. Garnish with bean sprouts, crushed peanuts, extra spring onion, a pinch of chilli flakes and a lime wedge.

Notes

  • The sauce should be a little thick, not watery — add extra tamarind paste if needed before tossing through the noodles.
  • Great for meal prep — divide into containers and reheat in the microwave or a pan.
  • No swaps needed here — no coconut milk or cream in this recipe.
