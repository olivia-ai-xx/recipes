Sticky Hoisin Beef

↗ View original recipe
Servings
2
Time
20 min
Main Ingredients
Beef mince, rice noodles, hoisin
Calories
~695 kcal / serve
🥬 Ingredients
Stir-Fry
  • 100g wide flat rice noodles
  • ½ tsp sesame oil, to toss through the noodles
  • ½ tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ tbsp freshly minced garlic
  • 1 spring onion, finely sliced (white and green parts separated)
  • 250g beef mince
  • ½ carrot, peeled and julienned or cut into thin batons
  • ¼ wombok (napa) cabbage, finely shredded (roughly 4 cups)
Sauce
  • 30ml hoisin sauce
  • ½ tbsp tamari or all-purpose soy sauce
  • ½ tbsp dark soy sauce
  • ½ tbsp rice wine vinegar (can be substituted with white vinegar or lime juice)
  • ½ tbsp brown sugar
  • ½ tsp sesame oil
To Serve
  • 20g crushed roasted peanuts (optional, can be substituted with crispy shallots)
  • lime wedges
🍳 Recipe
  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions. Drain, rinse under cold water, then toss with the sesame oil to prevent sticking. Set aside. Serve hot or cold — for hot noodles, cook them after the beef (step 4) and skip rinsing in cold water.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large, deep, heavy-based frying pan over medium–high heat. Add the garlic and the white parts of the spring onion. Cook for 30 seconds until just softened and fragrant. Add the beef mince and cook, breaking it up, for 5–7 minutes until browned.
  3. Add the carrot and cabbage and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the hoisin sauce, tamari, dark soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, brown sugar and sesame oil. Cook for 2–3 minutes until browned and sticky.
  5. Divide the noodles between bowls, top with the sticky beef mixture, crushed peanuts, the green parts of the spring onion, and serve with lime wedges.

Notes

  • Regular beef mince keeps this rich and saucy — swap in a leaner mince to reduce the fat content if preferred.
  • The beef and vegetable mixture can be made up to 3 days ahead and reheated; the noodles are best cooked fresh or stored separately.
