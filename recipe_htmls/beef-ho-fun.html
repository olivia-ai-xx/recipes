🥩 Beef

Beef Ho Fun

↗ View original recipe
Servings
2
Time
~40 min
Main Ingredients
Flank steak, rice noodles, Chinese broccoli, bean sprouts
Calories
~535 kcal / serve
🥬 Ingredients
Beef Marinade
  • 250g lean flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ tbsp oyster sauce
  • ½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp baking soda
Sauce
  • ½ tsp sesame oil
  • 1¼ tbsp oyster sauce
  • ½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • ½ tbsp coconut sugar
  • ½ tbsp Shaoxing wine (Chinese cooking wine)
  • 1½ tbsp water
Stir Fry
  • ½ tbsp neutral oil
  • ½ tbsp ginger paste
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 300g fresh rice noodles (or 110g dried rice noodles)
  • 2 cups Chinese broccoli, chopped (stems and leaves separated)
  • ½ cup bean sprouts
  • ¼ cup spring onions, sliced
To Serve
  • Chilli oil, to taste (optional)
  • Chilli flakes, to taste (optional)
🍳 Recipe
  1. Marinate the beef for 15 minutes. In a small bowl, mix the sauce ingredients and set aside. Prepare the rice noodles according to packet instructions.
  2. Heat a pan over high heat. Sear the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes until browned and slightly charred, letting it sit undisturbed for 1 minute before flipping. Remove from the pan.
  3. Add the oil, then the onion, garlic, and ginger. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Add the Chinese broccoli stems and cook for 2 minutes, then add the leaves and cook a further 1–2 minutes until wilted. Remove from the pan.
  4. Add the noodles to the hot pan and let sit undisturbed for 30 seconds to lightly char. Toss gently, being careful not to break the noodles, then add the sauce and cook for 1 minute until evenly coated.
  5. Return the beef and vegetables to the pan and toss for 1–2 minutes until glossy and combined. Add the bean sprouts and spring onions in the final 30 seconds. Serve immediately with chilli oil and chilli flakes, if desired.

Notes

  • Fresh rice noodles give the best charred wok texture — look for them in the fridge section of an Asian grocer.
  • If using dried rice noodles, rehydrate per packet instructions before stir-frying.
  • Cook the beef and noodles in batches if your pan isn't large enough to keep everything in one hot layer — crowding steams instead of chars.
  • Leftovers freeze well: portion once cooled and reheat in a hot pan or wok.
