Ajvar Pork Meatball Pitas

🐷 Pork · File: recipe_htmls/ajvar-pork-meatball-pitas.html

  • Servings: 2
  • Time: 30 mins
  • Main Ingredients: Pork Mince, Ajvar, Pita
  • Calories: ~700 kcal / serve

Macros (Per serving, based on 2 serves. Estimated using Woolworths AU ingredients.): 700 Calories · 47g Protein · 61g Carbs · 29g Fat

Ingredients

Pork Meatballs

  • 300g lean pork mince
  • 1 egg
  • 30g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika

Creamy Ajvar Sauce

  • 120g I Love Ajvar paprika relish
  • 80g Woolworths light Greek yogurt
  • 80ml chicken stock
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Cabbage Slaw

  • 150g green cabbage, thinly sliced
  • 60g carrot, grated
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp olive oil

To Serve

  • 2 Zeus Street Greek pita breads (Woolworths)
  • Fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Combine the pork mince, egg, panko breadcrumbs, garlic, oregano, parsley, smoked paprika, and a generous pinch of salt and pepper in a bowl. Mix until just combined — don't overwork. Shape into 12 even meatballs (about 40g each).
  2. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the meatballs for 10–12 minutes, turning every 2–3 minutes, until deep golden all over and cooked through with no pink inside.
  3. While the meatballs cook, make the creamy ajvar sauce: stir together the ajvar, light Greek yogurt, chicken stock, garlic, and lemon juice until smooth. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  4. Toss the cabbage and carrot together. Drizzle with lemon juice and olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss well to coat. Set aside — the slaw will soften slightly as it sits, which is ideal.
  5. Warm the pita breads in a dry pan for 1–2 minutes per side over medium heat until pliable and lightly charred, or wrap in foil and heat in the oven at 180°C for 5 minutes.
  6. To assemble: spread a generous spoonful of creamy ajvar sauce over each pita. Pile on the slaw, then the meatballs (6 per serve). Spoon over a little extra sauce, scatter with fresh parsley, and serve with lemon wedges.

Notes

  • Meatballs can be shaped a day ahead and refrigerated raw — cook straight from the fridge, adding 2 extra minutes to the cook time.
  • The creamy ajvar sauce doubles as a great dip or spread — make a double batch and keep refrigerated for up to 3 days.
  • Zeus Street Greek pita breads are stocked at Woolworths and are thicker and fluffier than standard flatbreads, making them ideal for loading up.

Bang Bang Shrimp

Ingredients

Prawns

  • 265g large prawns, peeled & deveined

Wet Batter

  • ⅔ cup buttermilk
  • ⅓ tbsp sriracha

Dry Batter

  • ⅔ cup cornstarch
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder
  • ⅔ tsp onion powder
  • ⅓ tsp MSG or salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Bang Bang Sauce

  • ⅓ cup mayo
  • 3 tbsp Thai sweet chilli sauce
  • 2½ tbsp sriracha
  • ⅔ tbsp lemon juice

To Serve

  • Steamed rice
  • Chives or spring onion
  • Chilli oil drizzle

Method

  1. Combine the buttermilk and sriracha in a bowl. Add the prawns, toss to coat and leave to soak for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix together the bang bang sauce ingredients in a separate bowl and set aside.
  3. Combine the cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder and salt in a shallow bowl for the dry batter.
  4. Remove prawns from the buttermilk and shake off excess. Coat in the cornstarch mixture and shake off any excess.
  5. Heat oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Fry the prawns for about 4 minutes until golden brown and crispy. Drain and allow to cool slightly.
  6. Toss the fried prawns in the bang bang sauce. Serve over steamed rice garnished with chives and a drizzle of chilli oil.

Notes

  • Don't skip the 20 minute buttermilk soak — it keeps the prawns tender inside.
  • Shake off excess batter well or it won't crisp up properly.
  • For a lighter version, air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes instead of deep frying.
  • The bang bang sauce makes more than needed — great as a dipping sauce or on other dishes.

  • ½ tbsp neutral oil

  • Leftovers freeze well: portion once cooled and reheat in a hot pan or wok.

Caramelized Soy Chicken in Garlic Ginger Broth with Rice

🍗 Chicken · File: recipe_htmls/caramelized-soy-chicken-broth.html · Source: https://simplyhomeflavors.com/caramelized-soy-chicken-in-garlic-ginger-broth-with-rice/

  • Calories: ~650 kcal / serve

Macros (Per serving, based on 2 serves. Estimated using Woolworths AU ingredients.): 650 Calories · 42g Protein · 55g Carbs · 24g Fat

Ingredients

Chicken & Broth

  • 350g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1½ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • ½ tbsp dark soy sauce (optional, for deeper colour)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 1½ tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • ½ tbsp rice vinegar (optional)
  • ½ tsp sesame oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

To Serve

  • 1 cup cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • Sesame seeds for garnish

Method

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, brown sugar, and dark soy sauce (if using). Stir until the sugar is dissolved. Set aside — this is your caramel sauce.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook for 4–5 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned on all sides but not fully cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, add the remaining ½ tbsp of oil. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the chicken broth along with the caramel sauce mixture. Stir well to combine and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Return the browned chicken to the pan, ensuring it's submerged in the broth. Cover with a lid, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 10–12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  6. Stir in the rice vinegar and sesame oil. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.
  7. Spoon cooked rice into bowls. Ladle the chicken and garlic ginger broth generously over the top.
  8. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds. Serve immediately.

Notes

  • Dark soy sauce is optional but adds beautiful colour and a slightly richer, maltier flavour — worth using if you have it.
  • Chicken thighs stay much juicier than breast in a brothy simmer — highly recommended.
  • Don't skip the browning step — that sear adds flavour depth that makes the broth far more complex.
  • Add sliced bok choy or mushrooms to the broth in the last 5 minutes for extra veg.
  • Leftovers keep well for 3 days — the broth soaks into the rice beautifully when reheated.

Ingredients

Broth & Noodles

  • 100g flat thin rice noodles
  • 1 litre low-sodium beef stock
  • 2 garlic cloves, peeled and bruised
  • ½ thumb-sized piece fresh ginger, roughly sliced
  • 1 star anise
  • 2 cloves
  • ½ tbsp fish sauce
  • ½ tbsp brown sugar
  • 150g beef eye fillet (tenderloin), very finely sliced

To Serve

  • 1 handful bean sprouts
  • ¼ bunch coriander
  • ½ lime, sliced into wedges
  • ½ red onion, halved and thinly sliced
  • 30ml hoisin sauce
  • Sriracha sauce (optional)
  • Sliced fresh chilli (optional)
  • Sliced spring onion (optional)

Method

  1. Cook the rice noodles according to packet instructions, reducing the cooking time by 2 minutes. Rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process, then drain and set aside.
  2. In a large pot, combine the beef stock, garlic, ginger, star anise, cloves, fish sauce and brown sugar. Bring to the boil, then reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 18 minutes. Remove and discard the garlic and ginger with a slotted spoon.
  3. Drop the thinly sliced beef into the simmering broth for 30 seconds. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside on a plate — it will finish cooking in the hot broth when served.

  • Slice the beef as thinly as possible (1–2mm). Partially freezing it for 30 minutes first makes this much easier.
  • Beef eye fillet or Scotch fillet give the most tender results. Porterhouse or rump work too but will have a firmer texture.
  • The broth and noodles can be prepared up to 2 days ahead — store separately in the fridge and reheat the broth before serving.
  • Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 2 days. Store noodles separately where possible. Not suitable for freezing.

Citrusy Lemon Butter Pan Fried Cod + Coconut Rice

Ingredients

Coconut Rice

  • ½ cup basmati or jasmine rice
  • ½ tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup water
  • ½ can (200ml) full-fat coconut milk
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • ½ tbsp unsalted butter

Lemon Butter Sauce

  • Zest of ½ lemon
  • 1½ tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter

Pan Fried Cod

  • 225g cod fillets (1–1.5″ thick)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

To Garnish

  • Fresh cilantro (coriander)
  • Pickled red onions
  • Cracked black pepper

Method

  1. Start the coconut rice: combine the rice and olive oil in a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 2–3 minutes until the rice is translucent and lightly golden. Add the water (it will bubble quickly), then stir in the coconut milk and salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to the lowest heat, cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes until all liquid is absorbed.
  2. Once the rice is cooked, remove the lid, fluff with a fork, and stir in the butter. Cover and let steam for a further 5–10 minutes while you cook the fish.
  3. Pat the cod fillets completely dry with paper towel on all sides. Season generously with salt and pepper on both sides, then dust lightly with flour, shaking off any excess.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat for 1 minute until glistening and hot. Carefully lay the cod fillets away from you into the pan. Cook untouched for 3–4 minutes per side until opaque and flaking easily. Transfer to a warm plate.

  6. Serve the cod over the coconut rice, drizzle generously with the lemon butter sauce, and garnish with fresh cilantro, pickled red onions, and cracked black pepper.

Notes

  • Pat the cod very dry before cooking — excess moisture will cause it to steam rather than sear, preventing that golden crust.
  • Don't move the fish once it's in the pan. Let it cook undisturbed so it develops a proper crust and releases naturally.
  • Frozen cod works fine — just thaw completely overnight in the fridge and pat dry very thoroughly before cooking.
  • Halibut or tilapia are great substitutes if cod isn't available.
  • The fat count is higher due to the full-fat coconut milk and butter — swap lite coconut milk to bring it down significantly.

🐷 Pork · File: recipe_htmls/vietnamese-caramelized-pork-bowls.html · Source: https://recipeamelia.com/vietnamese-caramelized-pork-bowls/

  • Servings: 2
  • Time: ~25 mins
  • Main Ingredients: Pork, Rice, Fish Sauce
  • Calories: ~590 kcal / serve

Macros (Per serving, based on 2 serves. Estimated using Woolworths AU ingredients.): 590 Calories · 32g Protein · 52g Carbs · 24g Fat

Ingredients

Caramelized Pork

  • 225g (½ lb) ground pork
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • ½ tbsp fish sauce
  • ½ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp water
  • ½ tbsp vegetable oil
  • ½ small onion, finely chopped
  • 1½ cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp fresh ginger, minced
  • Pinch crushed red pepper (optional)

To Serve

  • 1 cup cooked jasmine rice
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Sliced green onions
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the brown sugar, fish sauce, soy sauce, and water. Set aside — this is your caramel sauce.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook for about 3 minutes until translucent.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger, cooking for another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the ground pork to the skillet. Cook, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon, until fully browned.
  5. Pour the sauce over the cooked pork. Stir to combine and simmer for 3–5 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly and caramelizes, coating the pork beautifully.
  6. If using, stir in the crushed red pepper for a spicy kick.
  7. Serve over warm jasmine rice. Garnish with fresh cilantro, sliced green onions, and a generous squeeze of lime.

Notes

  • Don't rush the caramelizing step — those 3–5 minutes of simmering make all the difference for sticky, glossy pork.
  • Fish sauce is key to the authentic Vietnamese flavour; don't skip it.
  • Swap jasmine rice for vermicelli noodles or cauliflower rice for a lighter bowl.
  • Pickled cucumber or daikon makes a great fresh topping to balance the richness.
  • Leftovers reheat well — store pork separately from rice for best results.

Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken

🍗 Chicken · File: recipe_htmls/vietnamese-lemongrass-chicken.html · Source: https://www.culinaryaura.com/vietnamese-lemongrass-chicken/

  • Servings: 2
  • Time: ~20 mins + marinate
  • Main Ingredients: Chicken, Lemongrass, Fish Sauce
  • Calories: ~420 kcal / serve

Macros (Per serving, based on 2 serves (chicken only, not including rice). Estimated using Woolworths AU ingredients.): 420 Calories · 38g Protein · 12g Carbs · 24g Fat

Ingredients

Chicken & Marinade

  • 340g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1½ stalks fresh lemongrass, trimmed and finely minced (or 1½ tbsp lemongrass paste)
  • 1½ cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce for milder flavour)
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • ½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • ½ tbsp brown sugar
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper

To Cook

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • ½ small onion, finely sliced

To Serve

  • Steamed jasmine rice or vermicelli noodles
  • Sliced red chili (optional)
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Method

  1. In a bowl, combine the chicken pieces with lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, oyster sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, and black pepper. Mix well to coat. Cover and marinate for at least 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge for the best flavour.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the sliced onion and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the marinated chicken along with all the marinade to the pan. Stir-fry for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and caramelized around the edges.
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning — a little more fish sauce for saltiness, or a pinch of sugar for balance.
  5. Serve hot over jasmine rice or vermicelli noodles. Garnish with sliced chili, fresh cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

Notes

  • Marinating overnight makes a huge difference — the lemongrass flavour really penetrates the chicken.
  • Fresh lemongrass is best, but Woolworths lemongrass paste works well and saves prep time.
  • Chicken thighs are ideal here — they stay juicy and caramelize beautifully. Breast will work but cook it carefully to avoid drying out.
  • This dish is equally delicious served at room temperature as part of a fresh noodle salad.
  • Add a sliced bird's eye chili to the stir-fry if you like real heat.
